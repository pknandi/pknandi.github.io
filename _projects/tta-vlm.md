**Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS)** · July 2025 – present · Advisors: Dr. A K M Mahbubur Rahman, Dr. Md Mofijul Islam

This project studies test-time adaptation methods for vision–language models, so that the models stay reliable when the data they see at inference differs from their training data.
