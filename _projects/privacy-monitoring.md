**Therap (BD) Ltd.** · Sep 2021 – Sep 2024

- Designed and built a computer vision system that monitors patient activity remotely while protecting privacy, running on NVIDIA Jetson devices (Jetson NX, AGX Orin) with DeepStream.
- Contributed to a video redaction pipeline that uses person detection, instance segmentation, and multi-object tracking to redact individuals precisely.
- Devised algorithms that aggregate and statistically analyze model outputs into real-time, high-level activity data.
- Researched video-understanding and vision–language models and wrote recommendations to guide model selection.
- Co-inventor on a granted U.S. patent for non-invasive monitoring and redaction of personally identifiable information.

**Stack:** YOLO, pose estimation, face recognition, segmentation models, FastAPI, React, Docker, Kafka
