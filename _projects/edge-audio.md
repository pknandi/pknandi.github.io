**Therap (BD) Ltd.** · Oct 2024 – present

- **Audio classification:** Built a spectrogram-based model that detects sounds in medical cabins and care facilities. I compared signal-transformation methods, recorded real-world audio with a Raspberry Pi 5 and mini microphones, and refined preprocessing to reduce false positives.
- **Real-time speech-to-text:** Implemented live transcription of audio streams with OpenAI Whisper, captured through a microphone on a Raspberry Pi, and tuned preprocessing to reduce latency and transcription errors.
