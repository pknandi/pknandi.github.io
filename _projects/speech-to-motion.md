**Center for Computational & Data Sciences (CCDS)** · April 2026 – present · Advisors: Dr. A K M Mahbubur Rahman, Dr. Md Mofijul Islam

- Developing a **causal multimodal generative model** that produces continuous 3D co-speech motion from streaming audio, without access to future audio.
- Investigating **knowledge distillation** and inference strategies that improve motion realism and speech alignment under streaming constraints.
- Designing **latency audits and evaluation protocols** that measure audio look-ahead, temporal alignment, and motion continuity during online generation.
